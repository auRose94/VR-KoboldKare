# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community

### The adult-content context (adapted for this project)

KoboldKare is an adult game, and this mod touches its adult systems directly —
its code, logs, configs, and issue reports necessarily contain anatomical and
mature-fantasy terminology. This project applies the Contributor Covenant with
the following project-specific interpretation:

* **Allowed:** clinical, mechanical, and diagnostic use of the game's adult
  terminology in issues, discussions, commits, code, and documentation — when
  it describes the behavior of the game or these mods (a bug report about
  digestion timing, a log line naming an interaction, a config option for an
  adult system). This content is a normal part of working on the software.
* **Required:** such language stays *diagnostic* — pointed at issues,
  discussions, and the specific mechanics of the game and mod. It must not be
  *directed*: do not sexualize other members, their avatars, or real people;
  do not use the game's content as a vehicle for harassment, advances, or
  roleplay aimed at another participant.
* **Audience-aware writing:** the repo is public and indexed. Keep explicit
  detail to what the report actually needs, and keep issue titles and commit
  messages professional — someone should be able to read the tracker in a
  shared space without surprise.

Everything else in the Standards below applies unchanged.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment, including sexual or romantic advances toward
  other members using the game's or the mod's content
* Directed (non-diagnostic) sexual content aimed at another participant,
  including their in-game avatar
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issue tracker,
discussions, pull requests, and any project chat), and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at the contact
details in the project README. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.
